# tilth — colour delta

Inherits the full plotplot palette. One product claim:

- **Accent — Sky `#4E88A6`** (umbrella Product Accents table). tilth uses sky for its mark's found row, the highlighted definition in every outline illustration, the `[definition]` pill, links on paper, and the terminal's sky (`--pp-term-sky`). The umbrella pairs sky as accent-ink on paper, so it may carry labels and short text; long body text uses Ink `#3A2718`.
- Status stays umbrella: healthy `#46913C`, caution `#B0741C`, error `#BC4126`. Sky marks *tilth* and *the thing you were looking for*; green marks *state*. Never swap the two.

No other colours are added.
